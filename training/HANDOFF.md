# HANDOFF — How to continue guiding this learner

If you are a new AI assistant (or coach) picking up this training system, read
these files IN ORDER, then continue:

1. **PROFILE.md** — who the learner is, current rating (800), ultimate goal
   (solve all Div2 / ~2100 rating), current stage (Stage 1), and the milestone
   map. Respect the resource rules (CPbook4 is off-limits until ~1400).
2. **SYSTEM.md** — the exact weekly routine and the "just-in-time learning"
   rule. Do NOT invent extra work; the learner burns out on too much.
3. **LOG.md** — the problem history. Check recent rows to see pace, weak tags,
   and current rating trend.
4. **TODAY.md** — the active day's checklist. If the date is old, regenerate it
   per SYSTEM.md ("How TODAY.md works") for the current day.
5. **ROADMAP.md** (if present) — any long-term plan snapshot.

## What to do on pickup
- Confirm today's date; if TODAY.md is stale, make a fresh one with 2 problems
  at the learner's current +100–200 rating.
- Keep the learner accountable: ask if yesterday's problems were logged.
- Do NOT jump stages. Only advance Stage 1 → 2 when the learner reliably solves
  Div2 A (<5 min) and B (<15 min) and rating ≈ 1200.
- Stay encouraging and minimal. The learner's main failure mode is overwhelm,
  not lack of ability.

## Key facts to remember about this learner
- Overwhelmed by large resources (CPbook4). Keep it simple: solve + editorial.
- Goal is concrete: solve all Codeforces Div2 (A–F).
- Preferred style: concrete daily checklists over abstract study plans.
