# Training System — Weekly Routine & Rules

This is the ONLY routine the learner follows right now. Do not add extras.

## Weekly plan (repeat every week)
- **Mon–Fri:** 2 problems/day from Codeforces, rating **900–1100**.
  - Stuck >30 min → read editorial, understand fully, close it.
  - Next morning: re-solve from memory (no peeking).
- **Sat:** 1 virtual Div2 round OR solve A+B of a past Div2 round under timer.
- **Sun:** rest, or upsolve problems missed during the week.

## The core rule (beats overwhelm)
Do NOT study topics ahead of time. When a problem needs a technique you don't
know, learn ONLY that one technique (from CP4 chapter summary, USACO Guide, or
editorial), then move on.

## Problem selection
- Source: Codeforces problemset, filter by rating 900–1100.
  URL pattern: https://codeforces.com/problemset?order=BY_RATING_ASC
- Prefer problems tagged "implementation", "math", "greedy", "brute force"
  during Stage 1.

## Progress tracking
- Every solved/attempted problem goes into LOG.md (problem | rating | solved? |
  technique learned | date).
- Review LOG.md weekly to spot weak topics.

## How "TODAY.md" works
- TODAY.md holds the current day's checklist. At the start of each new day,
  regenerate it: copy the template, set the date, list 2 problems (or the
  Sat/Sun task). Mark items `[ ]` → `[x]` as done.
- Keep only ONE active TODAY.md (overwrite daily, history lives in LOG.md).

## Definition of done for Stage 1
Learner can solve Div2 A in <5 min and B in <15 min reliably, AND rating
reaches ~1200. Then advance to Stage 2 (update PROFILE.md stage + routine).
